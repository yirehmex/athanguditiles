Executive Summary

The Azure Coastal Villa project represents a synthesis of modern Mediterranean architecture and ancient Chettinad craft. Our artisanal scope focused on the fabrication of over 4,500 square feet of bespoke cement tiles, utilizing a unique pigment calibration to match the varying gradients of the Tyrrhenian Sea. Every tile was hand-cast by master artisans using traditional brass frames and locally sourced mineral pigments.

Artisanal Journey (Studio Logs)

Milestone 01: Pigment Calibration

Achieving the 'Cerulean Blue Gradient' required 14 iterations of mineral pigment mixing. We utilized cobalt oxide and natural lime to ensure UV stability against coastal sun exposure.

Milestone 02: Curing Cycle #3

The atmospheric curing phase is critical. Tiles are kept on stone surfaces for 21 days to achieve maximum hardness. Monitoring of humidity levels (65-70%) ensures structural integrity.
