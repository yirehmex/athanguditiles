Craftsmanship

How Every Tile Is Handmade

No machines. No kilns. Each tile is a small work of art, made by hand the same way it has been for generations.

🏺

Mix Materials

White cement, sand, and water mixed to precise ratios by experienced craftsmen.

🎨

Apply Color

Pure mineral pigments poured into a brass mold frame to form the design.

🔨

Press & Set

A hydraulic press compacts the tile under 200 tonnes of pressure for density.

☀️

Sun Dry

Tiles cure in open sunlight for 28 days — no kiln needed, zero carbon emission.

✨

Polish & Pack

Hand-polished to a smooth finish, quality-checked, and packed for shipping.

Athangudi tiles are best suited for dry floors, living rooms, courtyards, and walls. For wet areas like bathrooms, they can be used with proper sealing treatment. We advise consulting our team for wet-area applications.

Clean with a mild soap solution. Avoid acidic cleaners. Apply linseed oil once a year for the first 3 years to maintain the sheen. Tiles naturally become shinier with foot traffic and age — unlike ceramic tiles that fade.
