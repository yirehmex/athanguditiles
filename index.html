Our Story

Founded by the Durai brothers in 2023, YirehMex brings the ancient art of Athangudi tile-making to the modern world. Our name, YIREH, comes from Genesis 22:14 and means "The LORD our provider".

Our factory stands proudly on Athangudi Road in Chockalingampudur, Karaikudi, Tamil Nadu.

"We preserve traditional craftsmanship while serving customers worldwide with tiles that last 60-70 years."

Our Crafting Process

Each tile is a masterpiece, handcrafted through a time-honored 6-step process

STEP 1 – Blending
High-quality cement, Athangudi sand, and natural pigments are blended.
STEP 2 – Molding
Poured into brass molds on glass with design precision.
STEP 3 – Pressing
Manual press creates strong compact structure.
STEP 4 – Curing
Water curing + air drying ensures durability.
STEP 5 – Finishing
Surface removed and edges polished perfectly.
STEP 6 – Quality Check
Each tile inspected to meet standards.
